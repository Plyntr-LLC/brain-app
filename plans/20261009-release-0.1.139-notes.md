Sign-in pop-ups, camera and microphone asks, and Touch ID passkeys in Brain's browser.

- Sign-in pop-ups work. "Sign in with Google" or "Pay with PayPal" windows open on top of the page in the browser picture and hand the result back when they close. A Close pop-up button sits next to Reload. A link clicked inside WhatsApp now opens on top too, so WhatsApp stays where it was.
- Pages only open pop-ups right after you or the AI click something, three at most. Ads can't stack windows by themselves.
- Camera, microphone and clipboard. When a page asks, a card under the picture asks you: Allow or Don't allow. Brain remembers your Allow for the camera and microphone on that site, but uses it only while you're watching the page. Clipboard reads ask every time.
- Touch ID passkeys. Sites can save a passkey in Brain and sign you in with Touch ID. Brain only lets a site ask while you're watching the page, and a card lets you cancel. These passkeys live in Brain on this Mac. Your iCloud Keychain passkeys aren't available in Brain yet.
- Printing from a page always works now, including receipts a site prints from a hidden frame. You get a PDF in Downloads instead of a page that stops responding.
