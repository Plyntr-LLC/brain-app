0.1.139
Brain's browser now does what a browser does. You can sign in through pop-ups, upload and download files, print, and use Touch ID passkeys.

- Browse on your own. Back, Forward, Reload and an address bar sit under the picture. Type an address, or a few words to search.
- Sign-in pop-ups work. "Sign in with Google" opens on top of the page and hands you back when it's done. Close pop-up is next to Reload. Links in WhatsApp open on top too, so WhatsApp stays put.
- Files go both ways. A page that wants a file shows you a normal Open panel, and only you pick. Downloads show a line with Open and Show in Finder.
- Printing works. Cmd+P or a page's own Print button saves a PDF to Downloads, receipts included.
- You decide on camera, microphone and clipboard. A card under the picture asks first. Brain remembers your camera and microphone answer per site, and uses it only while you're watching.
- Touch ID passkeys. A site can save a passkey in Brain and sign you in with your finger, only while you're watching the page. Your iCloud Keychain passkeys don't work in Brain yet.
- Brain now needs macOS 13 or later. Older Macs stay on 0.1.137.
