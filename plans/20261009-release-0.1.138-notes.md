0.1.138
Back, Forward, Reload and an address bar in Brain's browser, plus uploads, downloads, printing, sound and a right-click menu. Brain now runs on Electron 44 and needs macOS 13 or later.

- Browse on your own. Under the browser picture, after the sizes, there's now Back, Forward, Reload and an address bar. Type an address, or a few words to search Google. On WhatsApp, Back and Forward stay off so no chat can move WhatsApp off its page.
- Upload files. When a page asks for a file, Brain shows a normal Open panel that names the site. Only you pick the files; the AI can click the upload button but cannot choose files.
- Downloads you can find. A finished download shows a line under the picture with Open and Show in Finder. A download that breaks off says so.
- Print. Cmd+P on a Wide or Large picture, or Print to PDF in the right-click menu, saves the page as a PDF in Downloads and opens it.
- Right-click menu. In a Wide or Large picture: Back, Forward, Reload, copy a link, open a link in your own browser, copy and paste in fields, copy an image address, print.
- Sound. A page plays sound while you're watching it in Wide or Large, and is quiet otherwise.
- Electron 44. Brain moves from Electron 37 to 44. Macs older than macOS 13 (Ventura) stay on 0.1.137 and are not offered this update.
